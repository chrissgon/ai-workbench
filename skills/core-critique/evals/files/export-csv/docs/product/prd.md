|----|---------|-------|
| F-6 | Suggest the client's approved term while the translator types | P-1 |

| F-8 | Bring a glossary in from a file | P-3 |

## F-7, take a glossary out

Translators hand a client's glossary to agencies that do not use Lexiloom. The export keeps every term,
its translation and its note, and only a member who can read the glossary can take it out.
