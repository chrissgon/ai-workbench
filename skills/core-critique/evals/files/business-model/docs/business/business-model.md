# Business model: Lexiloom

- Owner: biz-business-model
- Status: draft
- Date: 2026-04-14

## Summary

Lexiloom is a web application where freelance translators keep one glossary per client and get the
client's approved term suggested while they type. This document must show that Lexiloom can reach
400 paying subscribers within 12 months of launch, the number the founders set as the bar for
continuing after the first year.

## Customer

Freelance translators who work for three or more direct clients and keep their terminology in
spreadsheets today.

## Market

The market is worth 2 billion per year. The figure is the size of the global translation software
market in an industry analyst's 2025 report; freelancers are part of that market.

## Competition

There are no direct competitors. No product is built only for a freelancer's per-client glossaries.

## Revenue

- One plan: 49 per month, billed monthly, no free tier.
- 400 subscribers × 49 = 19,600 of monthly recurring revenue at month 12.

## Channels

- A weekly article on terminology management, published on the Lexiloom blog.
- Two translator conferences in the first year, with a stand at each.

## Costs

- Hosting and tooling: 600 per month.
- Two founders, unpaid in the first year.
- Conferences: 4,000 each.

## Assumptions

- none recorded
