# Pricing proposal: Pennyfold Plus

- Author: Tomas Reyes
- Date: 2027-10-12

## Goal

Reach 400 paying members by the end of the first year after launch, without raising the churn of the free
plan above 6% a month.

## Proposal

- Free plan: one wallet, 12 months of history.
- Plus: 4 EUR a month or 40 EUR a year; unlimited wallets, full history, CSV export, shared wallets.
- Launch offer: the first 3 months of Plus at 1 EUR a month for members who join in the launch week.

## Why

- Two of the three expense trackers our interviewees used charge between 3 and 5 EUR a month.
- 11 of 20 interviewees said they would pay for shared wallets.
