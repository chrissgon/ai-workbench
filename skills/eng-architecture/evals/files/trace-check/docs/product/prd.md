# Product requirements: Fennel Books

- Owner: product-prd
- Status: approved
- Date: 2026-04-02

## Summary

Fennel Books keeps the expenses of small associations and hands them to their accountant.

## Users

- U-1: the association's treasurer, who enters expenses every week.
- U-2: the accountant, who receives the expenses once a month and loads them into an accounting import tool.

## Features

- F-1: Enter an expense (date, payee, category, amount). Phase P-1.
- F-2: List the expenses of one month on the Expenses page. Phase P-1.
- F-3: Categories the treasurer can rename. Phase P-1.
- F-4: Monthly export: the treasurer downloads one month of expenses as a file for the accountant. Phase P-1.

## Out of scope

- Bank connections; receipts; more than one currency.
