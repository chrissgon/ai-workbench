- Owner: mkt-messaging
- Status: approved

## Positioning

For people who keep houseplants and forget to water them, Fernleaf is a reminder app that knows each plant's own schedule.

## Landing page (SCREEN-1)

- SECTION-1: Hero. Headline: Every plant, watered on its own schedule. Body: Fernleaf learns how often each of your plants needs water and reminds you on the right day. CTA: Get Fernleaf.
- SECTION-2: Benefits. Headline: Less guessing, fewer dry leaves. Body: One schedule per plant; a reminder only on the days it matters; a log of every watering.
- SECTION-3: Screenshot. Headline: Your week of watering at a glance. Body: The schedule shows which plants need water today and which can wait.

## Words

- Use: water, schedule, reminder, each plant.
- Avoid: smart, AI-powered, revolutionary.
