# Profile: mockup (the product shown in context: device, browser, print, environment)

The Content section must contain:

- **Scene:** what is shown and where (a laptop on a desk, a browser window floating on the brand background, a phone in hand), the camera angle and the mood.
- **Placement:** which screen or asset of the product appears in the scene, attached as a file, never redrawn by the tool.
- **Sizes:** output sizes and formats (for example 1600 × 1000 PNG, 1080 × 1350 for a social post) and whether a transparent background is needed.

Criteria that matter for mockups:

- The product's screen is the real one, legible, not re-imagined by the tool.
- Background, light and props use the brand's colours and do not compete with the screen.
- The scene says one thing about the product (lightweight, fast, elegant) and nothing that the product does not do.
- Output sizes and formats as listed.
- No third-party logos or trademarks in the scene.

Deliverables: round 1, one scene per direction at one size; round 2, every size of the chosen scene.
