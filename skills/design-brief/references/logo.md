# Profile: logo (mark, wordmark, lockup)

The Content section must contain:

- **Name:** the exact name as it must be written (case, spacing), and a one-line meaning or story the mark may draw on.
- **Variants:** mark alone, wordmark, horizontal and stacked lockups, monochrome, on light and on dark backgrounds.
- **Minimum size:** the smallest size each variant must stay legible at (for example the mark at 16 px as a favicon, the wordmark at 80 px wide).
- **Clear space:** the empty area around the logo, as a proportion of the mark.
- Existing marks to keep, evolve or replace, stated as the user decided.

Criteria that matter for logos:

- Recognisable at the minimum size and in one colour.
- Works on both backgrounds without an outline hack.
- Distinct from the direct competitors' marks (name them).
- Built from simple geometry that can be drawn as a clean vector; no gradients or effects that break in one colour.
- Says the product's one idea (for a lightweight library: restraint, minimal parts).

Deliverables: round 1, three to five marks per direction in black on white at large size and at 16 px; round 2, the full variant set of the chosen mark as vectors.
