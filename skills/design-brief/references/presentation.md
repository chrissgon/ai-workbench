# Profile: presentation (deck, pitch, talk, report)

The Content section must contain:

- **Format:** aspect ratio (16:9 by default), where it will be shown (projector, shared screen, PDF sent by email) and the target length (minutes or slide count).
- **Slides:** one line per slide in order: its single message (a sentence, verbatim from the source), the evidence or visual that carries it (chart with its data, screenshot, quote), and the speaker note if any.
- The data of every chart, written out with its source, so the tool does not invent numbers.

Criteria that matter for presentations:

- One message per slide, readable in five seconds; body text at least 24 pt on a 16:9 slide.
- Every number and chart traces to the data given; nothing invented.
- A visual system repeated across slides (title position, grid, colour roles), from the design system.
- Charts labelled directly, no legends to decode.
- The title slide and the closing slide carry the product's identity hooks.

Deliverables: round 1, the title slide and two content slides per direction; round 2, the full deck of the chosen direction, exported to the requested format.
