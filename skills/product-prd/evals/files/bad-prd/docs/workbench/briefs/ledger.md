# Brief: Ledger

- Status: approved by the user on 2026-09-22
- Topic: ledger

## Request, in the user's words

"Freelance designers I work with lose track of which invoices are overdue. I want a small web app that lists invoices and reminds them."

## Decisions

1. In scope for launch: a list of invoices with their status, and a reminder to the freelancer when an invoice is overdue. (user, 2026-09-22)
2. Out of scope: accounting exports. (user, 2026-09-22)
3. Web only. (user, 2026-09-22)
4. Audience: freelance designers who bill 3 to 8 clients a month. No other audience. (user, 2026-09-22)
5. A portal where the client pays online comes after launch, in a second phase called "Payments". (user, 2026-09-22)
6. Goal: every invoice is paid within its term. No target number and no launch date were set. (user, 2026-09-22)

## Not decided

- Which email provider sends the reminders.
- Any numeric target or date.
